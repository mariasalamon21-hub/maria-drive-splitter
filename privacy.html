Privacy Policy for Maria Splitter

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. About Maria Splitter

Maria Splitter is a browser-based tool that helps users split and merge local files and manage files in their Google Drive account.

2. Information and Permissions

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app requests permission to access your Google Drive files. This permission is used to display files and folders and to perform actions you request, such as uploading, copying, moving, or creating folders.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Google Drive information is accessed to provide the features you choose to use. Local file splitting and merging are performed in your browser when supported by your device and browser.

The app is hosted as a static website and does not operate its own server to store your Google Drive files. Google processes your account information and Drive content under Google's own privacy policies and terms.

4. Sharing and Selling Information

Maria Splitter does not sell your personal information. The app does not intentionally share your Google Drive files with the developer. Any file operations should be initiated by you through the app.

5. Data Security

Access to Google Drive is handled through Google's authorization system. You should only connect an account you control and review requested permissions before granting access.

6. Removing Access

You can revoke Maria Splitter's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account's third-party connections settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Maria Splitter is not designed specifically for children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email listed on the Maria Splitter app's Google authorization page.
