Maria Drive Splitter

Split large files into parts and optionally upload the parts to Google Drive.

☁️ Google Drive

Connect Google Drive only if you want to upload your split parts.
Merge does not use Google Drive.
Not connected

✂️ Split File

Select the original file directly from your device.
The original file stays on your device; it is not uploaded automatically.
Binary units are used: 1 KB = 1024 bytes, 1 MB = 1024² bytes, etc.

🔗 Merge Parts

Select the split parts directly from your device.
Google Drive is NOT used for merging.

After selecting the files, use ↑ and ↓ to put them in the order you want.
