Connect Google Drive to manage your cloud files.

My Drive
Connect your Google account to display files.

Select files using the checkboxes. Copy lets you choose files from Google Drive or your device. Paste lets you choose Google Drive or your device as the destination.

Split a local file

Choose a file from your device and save its parts into a local folder. The parts are not automatically uploaded to Drive.

Merge local parts

Choose the local folder containing files named like filename.ext.part001, filename.ext.part002, and so on.
